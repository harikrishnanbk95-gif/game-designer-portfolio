Design Breakdown

Four different genres demonstrate different approaches to systems and player interaction.

01 — Strategy

Iron Command: Siege Town

The core loop revolves around rebuilding the town, training units, defending against attacks, earning rewards, upgrading the base and army, then expanding. The concept also includes co-op and PvP multiplayer.

Core LoopBuild → Train → Defend → Upgrade
SystemsResources, units, walls, turrets
RetentionMissions, events, multiplayer
🏎️🌙
02 — Racing

Midnight Mini Racers

The game's identity comes from scale: tiny racers navigate oversized household environments while a sunrise timer adds pressure. Cars can be upgraded and customized with creative modifications.

HookTiny world perspective
PressureBeat the sunrise
ObstaclesPets and environment
🤸‍♂️🛒
03 — Physics

Ragdoll Jump Challenge

The player chooses a character and a vehicle or object such as a cycle, skateboard, shopping cart or office chair. After launching from a ramp, ragdoll mode takes over in the air and scoring rewards distance, flips and style.

👽🧸
04 — Stealth

Tiny Monster Escape

A toy-car-sized alien moves through short levels and must freeze like a toy whenever a human looks at it. Cars become fast hazards, and each level ends at a glowing spaceship.
